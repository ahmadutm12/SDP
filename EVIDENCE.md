## Decisions and their timing

- **Practical thresholds.** A12 ≥ 0.64 with |ΔMCC| ≥ 0.05 (and the mirror rule for decreases) were entered in the analysis after the results of the previous version existed. They were not pre-registered. They are justified as the conventional Vargha–Delaney medium effect and the MCC margin used in the earlier draft. `threshold_grid.csv` reports the counts for A12 ∈ {0.56, 0.64, 0.71} and |ΔMCC| ∈ {0.02, 0.05, 0.10}, with unpaired and paired A12.
- **Primary effect size.** Unpaired A12 is primary, for comparability with the previous version; paired A12 over the 50 shared folds is reported as a sensitivity analysis.
- **Corrected CI.** The corrected repeated-CV t interval was chosen in revision 1, after reviewer comments. For repeated k-fold CV it is an approximation.
- **RQ2 model.** A linear mixed model with a fold random effect was singular; cluster-robust OLS (clusters = dataset × repeat × fold) is used instead. p-values are descriptive.
- **Scope of sensitivity runs.** Selector-setting runs use DT, RF and XGBoost. Classifier-matched selection was run for IG with RF and XGBoost and for GEO with XGBoost; GEO with RF was not run, to limit compute. Inner RF and XGBoost use 100 trees.
- **Datasets.** No further datasets were added in revision 1; the manuscript states the scope and the risk of dataset-selection bias.

## Corrections made during revision 1

- The previous IG code ranked metrics and chose k on the whole outer training fold. This is fixed (nested selection). The effect on ΔMCC is listed above.
- The previous manuscript said the thresholds were fixed before the results were inspected. That statement was wrong and is removed.
- A first version of the stability table compared metric names case-sensitively. `pc1.arff` capitalises nine metric names (for example `iv(G)`, `N`), so PC1 subset sizes and Φ were wrong in that draft table (GEO PC1 Φ 0.338 and IG PC1 Φ 0.782 before the fix). The comparison is now case-insensitive and the values above are the corrected ones. Model fitting was not affected, because selection works on column indices.
