  results_rev1/, analysis tables and figures (make_tables_rev1.py, make_figures_rev1.py).

  Nogueira stability, train-vs-test MCC, RQ2 variance decomposition.
- Sensitivity: duplicates kept, IG bins 5/20, fixed k 5/10/15, GEO 20x50,
  classifier-matched selection, KC1 permutation importance, train/test overlap.
- verify_rerun.py and check_headlines.py for independent reruns; manifest_rev1.json
  records versions, seeds, data SHA-256 and output SHA-256.
